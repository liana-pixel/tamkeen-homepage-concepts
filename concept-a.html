What do you want to achieve?

We enable growth and prosperity for Bahrain and its people

Since 2006 we have been supporting the private sector to be the key engine of economic growth, and to empower Bahraini talent to be the first choice of employment in the labor market. These two mandates drive our support programs, partnerships, and initiatives.

We support enterprises to grow, transform, and innovate, and we help upskill and reskill local talent to become more productive and competitive in the workforce, locally and internationally.

Who is eligible for Tamkeen support?

Eligibility differs by program. Each program page lists the criteria for individuals and enterprises.

How do I apply for a program?

Choose a program in the Program Finder, review the requirements, then select Start now to begin your application.

How can I track my request?

Requests are handled through Tawasul, where you can follow the status of your inquiry.

Where can I find reports and user guides?

Reports, publications and step-by-step user guides are available in the Knowledge Center.
