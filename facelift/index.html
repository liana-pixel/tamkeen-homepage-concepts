About us

We enable growth and prosperity for Bahrain and its people.

Since 2006 we have been supporting the private sector to be the key engine of economic growth, and to empower Bahraini talent to be the first choice of employment in the Labour market. These two mandates have been the key drivers behind our support programs, partnerships, and initiatives, through which we support enterprises to grow, transform, and innovate and through which we help upskill and reskill local talent to become more productive and competitive in the workforce both on a local and international scale.

Find out more

Partnerships & Initiatives

Through Partnerships & Initiatives we aim to Inspire, Connect, and Grow.

Our strategic approach is based on Bahrain’s economic vision to empower the private sector and promote the participation of Bahraini talent towards economic growth. As a result, we offer various support programs and have partnered with reputable local and international organizations to help upskill, reskill, and elevate the local talent competencies, thus increasing their competitiveness in the labour market.
